abstract = "Third technical report of the TRAISIM project. It presents an Adaptive Model Selection (AMS) framework for operator training platforms. From the pre-disturbance operating point and the contingency alone, graph-based predictors identify which components will be dynamically active, and full node-breaker detail is kept only there. On the French transmission system, the selected formulation reduces continuous and discrete model size by about 72% and 66%. When the critical substations are retained, voltage trajectory error stays near the solver tolerance, with speedups up to about 3×."
